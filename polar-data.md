## Organisation

- **National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research (NCPOR)** — India's premier polar/ocean R&D institute.
- Established **25 May 1998** (originally as NCAOR) in **Vasco da Gama, Goa** (15.397°N, 73.793°E).
- Founding figure: Dr. Prem Chand Pandey.
- Autonomous institute under the **Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)**, Government of India.
- Administers India's Antarctic, Arctic, Southern Ocean and Himalayan cryosphere programmes.
- Manages the research vessel **ORV Sagar Kanya** (built Germany, 1983; 4,888 GT; 100.34 m length;
  studies the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean).

## Stations

### Dakshin Gangotri
- **Established:** 1983 — India's first Antarctic station.
- **Location:** Princess Astrid Coast, Queen Maud Land, Antarctica.
- **Status:** Decommissioned. Buried under accumulating ice/snow; abandoned operationally 1988–90,
  officially decommissioned 1990. Now a historical/heritage site and supply base.
- **Notes:** Built with an ice-melting plant, laboratories, storage, accommodation, clinic. Named after
  the Dakshin Gangotri glacier.

### Maitri
- **Established:** 1989.
- **Location:** Schirmacher Oasis, Queen Maud Land, Antarctica (near freshwater Lake Priyadarshini).
- **Status:** Active, year-round research station.
- **Research focus:** Geology, geomorphology, glaciology, medicine, atmospheric sciences.

### Bharati
- **Established:** 18 March 2012 — India's newest and third Antarctic station.
- **Location:** Larsemann Hills, East Antarctica. Coordinates approx. **69°S, 76°E**.
- **Status:** Active, year-round research station.
- **Notes:** Built from 134 recycled shipping containers; ~2,500 sq. m. Sits on an ice-free rock oasis.
  Research focus: oceanography, remote sensing, evidence of continental breakup (Gondwana geology).

### Himadri
- **Established:** Inaugurated **1 July 2008** — India's first Arctic research station.
- **Location:** Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard, Norway — the world's northernmost year-round research settlement,
  ~1,200 km from the North Pole.
- **Status:** Active.
- **Research focus:** Atmospheric sciences, glaciology, biological/marine sciences, space weather.
- **Related infrastructure:** IndARC (India's first Arctic underwater observatory, deployed 2014 in
  Kongsfjorden, Svalbard); Gruvebadet atmospheric laboratory access.

### Himansh (context — not a polar station but part of NCPOR's cryosphere network)
- **Established:** 2016.
- **Location:** Sutri Dhaka, Chandra basin, Himachal Pradesh, India — elevation ~4,080 m.
- **Purpose:** High-altitude Himalayan glaciology observatory.

## The Indian Antarctic Act, 2022

- **Act No. 13 of 2022**, assented by the President on **6 August 2022**, notified 8 August 2022.
- Gives domestic legal effect to India's obligations under the **Antarctic Treaty**, the **Convention on
  the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources (CCAMLR)**, and the **Protocol on Environmental
  Protection to the Antarctic Treaty**.
- Requires a **permit** for any Indian expedition, station, vessel, or aircraft activity in Antarctica.
- Prohibits nuclear explosions, radioactive waste disposal, mining/mineral exploitation, introduction of
  non-sterile soil, and harmful discharge into the sea.
- Establishes a **Committee on Antarctic Governance and Environmental Protection** for compliance,
  inspections, and waste-management oversight.

## Expedition Programme — key verified facts

- India's Antarctic programme began in **1981**. The first expedition departed Goa **6 December 1981**
  aboard **MV Polar Circle**, landed in Antarctica **9 January 1982**, led by **Dr. Sayed Zahoor Qasim**
  (former Director, CSIR-National Institute of Oceanography).
- As of 2026 the programme has run **over 44 expeditions** (44th ISEA launched Oct–Nov 2024; 45th ISEA
  launched Oct–Nov 2025), both themed **"Climate Change and its Signatures in Antarctica."**
- The programme is run jointly by NCPOR's **Antarctic Scientific Division** and **Antarctic Logistics
  Division**, with participating institutes across India (the Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica,
  ISEA, is a multi-institutional, multi-decade programme).

## Research Areas

- **Glaciology & Cryosphere Sciences** — ice cores, glacier mass balance, ice sheet dynamics, Himalayan
  and polar glaciers.
- **Atmospheric Sciences** — aerosols, Antarctic haze, ozone, polar boundary-layer studies.
- **Marine Biology & Biological Sciences** — Southern Ocean ecosystems, microbial communities in polar
  snow/ice, coral and isotope-based paleoclimate proxies.
- **Geology & Earth Sciences** — Gondwana continental reconstruction, geomorphology of ice-free oases
  (Schirmacher, Larsemann Hills).
- **Ocean Sciences** — Southern Ocean–Indian monsoon teleconnections, sea surface temperature
  reconstructions, physical and chemical oceanography aboard ORV Sagar Kanya.
